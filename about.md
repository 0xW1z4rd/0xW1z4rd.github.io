## Who I Am

I'm **Wizard**, a security researcher and detection engineer working at the intersection of offensive and defensive security. My work focuses on understanding attacker techniques at a deep level — then building the detection logic to catch them.

I spend my time on:

- Active Directory attack paths and post-exploitation techniques
- DFIR investigations and threat hunting
- Detection engineering (Sigma, Splunk, Elastic)
- Red team operations and adversary simulation
- CTF competitions

---

## What I Do

### Offensive Research

I research attack techniques across the kill chain — from initial access through domain compromise. My focus areas include:

- **Active Directory** — Kerberoasting, AS-REP Roasting, LLMNR/NBNS poisoning, delegation attacks, ACL abuse
- **Network attacks** — MITM, relay attacks, credential capture
- **Post-exploitation** — lateral movement, persistence, defense evasion

### Detection Engineering

I translate attack knowledge into detection logic. I work with:

- **Sigma** — vendor-agnostic detection rules
- **Splunk** — SPL queries and correlation rules
- **Elastic/ECS** — EQL and KQL detection
- **Suricata** — network-based detection

### DFIR

I conduct incident response investigations and forensic analysis, with experience in:

- Memory forensics and volatile data analysis
- Windows event log analysis
- Network traffic analysis and PCAP review
- Malware triage
